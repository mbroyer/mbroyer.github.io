MSTR

pronounced “muster”

Build beats in your browser. Eight tracks, sixteen steps, six effects, plus your own sound from the mic.

16 templates to start from, or begin with an empty grid.

Good to know

Do I need to sign up?

No. Just open it and start, no code and no account.

Which devices does MSTR run on?

In the browser on phone, tablet and computer, best with headphones.

Why does MSTR need the microphone?

Only for the Mic track, if you want to record your own sound. Everything else works without it.
