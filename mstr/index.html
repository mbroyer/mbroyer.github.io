Why

MSTR started as a little monster beatbox inside NumNum, a maths game I made for kids. The grown-ups kept stealing it. So I rebuilt it for them: no monsters, just beats.

What it’s for

Say how you feel and get a beat in seconds. Spin it in the Orbit, share it as a video, or open the machine and place every hit yourself.

Call it vibe drumming. No AI involved, just rules, synths and your mood.

Good to know

  • Free. No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Runs in the browser on phone, tablet and computer. Best with headphones.
  • The microphone is only for the Mic track. Your recording stays on your device.
